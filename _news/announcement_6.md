Selective prediction allows AI systems to abstain when outputs are unreliable. How can we move beyond binary risks while retaining finite-sample validity? Check out our new paper [SCoRE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24704), where we develop an e-value-based framework for general risk control in selective prediction.
